Every one of the 12 single-strategy sessions is net-negative (net R -0.08..-0.29), with

## Entry quality (H4: the same entries held to stop/target, or for 15 min / 1 h / 3 h)

Holding the IDENTICAL V1 entries longer does not reveal a hidden gross edge. For 83 of 84
H4 trials the 95 % interval of mean gross R contains 0 (or is below it). The one exception,
XAUUSD `momentum_continuation` FH12 (gross +0.144 R, CI [+0.016, +0.272], n 325), is what
chance alone produces about 4 times in 84 tests; its DSR within the family is 0.001 and its
net R is still -0.005. **The entries carry no demonstrable gross edge at any tested horizon.**

## Exit effect (H4 paired differences, same entry under two exits)

| Question | Answer |

The backtest's per-fill slippage assumption is about 4x (XAUUSD) and 2x (BTCUSD) the
DEMO-observed entry p90. A POST-HOC sensitivity that uses the observed entry p90 (exit
slippage unchanged) lowers cost by about 0.04-0.08 R per trade; **every V1 and selector
session stays net-negative** (e.g. XAUUSD selector -0.125, BTCUSD selector -0.227). The
assumption is NOT changed: exit slippage (stop exits are market orders) is unmeasured, and
lowering a cost assumption to create profit is forbidden. Cost stress: at cost x1.2 every
trial is worse (nothing is positive at x1.0 to begin with).

## Marginal future cost (H5)

V1's remaining-edge test does charge the full round-trip cost against the remaining
distance to target, but replacing it with the exit-side marginal cost changed **no exit in
any cohort** (V1-MC is identical to V1, MEV to THESIS). The test never binds before the
600 s max hold, the entry re-trigger or SL/TP. The double counting exists in code but is not
a cause of the losses.

## Turnover

`statistical_reversion` and `microstructure_acceleration` produce about 85 % of all V1 trades
(XAUUSD 1122 + 1323, BTCUSD 1150 + 860 as independent sessions; 89-93 % of selector trades
are `microstructure_acceleration`) with gross R about 0 and the highest cost R (0.20-0.24):
they are the largest loss contributors. The H2 friction filter (target >= 3x / 5x estimated
cost, cooldown) barely changed trade counts because V1 targets already exceed those
multiples: the filter does not bind. The problem is the absence of gross edge, not a tail of
marginal-cost trades.

## Calibration and selector (H3)

Raw confidence is not a probability. Out-of-fold, realized net win rates are 0.15-0.52 for
raw confidence 0.41-0.99, with no monotone relation; gross R predicted from earlier folds
changes sign in the later folds in most bins. The calibrated expected-value selector (H3)
therefore correctly abstained on every bar (0 trades, both margins): FLAT is the right
decision when no strategy shows calibrated positive expectancy.

## Strategy, symbol, direction, session, regime (descriptive; any idea here is POST-HOC)

- Strategy: no strategy is net-positive on either symbol under any exit. Least negative:
  `momentum_continuation` and `pullback_continuation` (net -0.04..-0.10 with longer holds).
- Symbol: XAUUSD is less negative than BTCUSD (selector net -0.19 vs -0.27). Neither is positive.
- Direction: small, opposite asymmetries (XAUUSD SELL better, BTCUSD BUY better): noise level.
- Session (selector entries held to SL/TP): XAUUSD London/NY overlap 12-16 UTC is the only
  net-positive session (n 170, gross +0.171, net +0.058). One subgroup among 10
  session x symbol cells, found after the fact: not evidence, only a candidate hypothesis.
- Regime: 91-95 % of entries occur in RANGE; the other regimes have too few trades.
- Volatility / cost-to-risk: net R is far better when the trade's spread cost is small
  relative to its initial risk (lowest spread-cost tercile: XAUUSD -0.04, BTCUSD -0.06, versus
  highest -0.30 / -0.35) and, for XAUUSD, in the high-volatility tercile (-0.03 vs -0.32 low).
  Losses concentrate where stops are tight relative to friction.

## Root cause (section 20)

| Cause | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A. poor entries | **Primary.** No gross edge at any horizon (H4). |
| B. poor exits / C. holding logic | Not the cause. Alternative exits and holding theses do not change gross R materially (H1, H4). |
| D. transaction costs | **Primary amplifier.** Cost 0.10-0.24 R per trade on a gross edge of about 0; stops are tight relative to spread + slippage. Slippage assumption conservative on the entry side (DEMO), unknown on the exit side. |
| E. selector miscalibration | Present (raw confidence is uncalibrated) but secondary: a calibrated selector finds nothing to select. |
| F. too many marginal trades | Present: two high-frequency, zero-edge strategies generate most trades and most losses. Filtering by cost multiple does not help because the trades are not marginal on cost, they lack edge. |

## Candidate

**None.** No trial meets the development criteria (net R > 0, gross >= 1.5x cost, >= 8/12
folds, PSR/DSR >= 0.95, PBO <= 0.20, >= 100 trades, positive at cost x1.2). Family PBO:
XAUUSD H4 0.05 / H5 0.15, BTCUSD H4 0.00 / H5 0.00 (low PBO here only means the ranking of
uniformly losing variants is stable). Nothing is locked; OOS remains sealed.

## Next steps (to be pre-registered before any run; none has been run)

1. **Measure exit-side execution cost in DEMO** (the one unobserved cost component that
   dominates the model). This needs a runtime observability change (record exit fill vs
   decision price per close), made only through the normal release process, never under a
   running process.
2. **H6, cost-to-risk and horizon:** entries only where the estimated round-trip cost is a

   longer bar resolution (M15/H1) with correspondingly wider stops. Rationale: every
   segment analysis points at cost relative to stop as the lever; M5 scalping with tight
   stops cannot clear the observed friction.
3. **H7, XAUUSD London/NY overlap** (POST-HOC origin, declared as such): session-restricted
   momentum/pullback entries, pre-registered, counted in the family, judged only on data
   not used to form it (a later development window), never on the OOS.
4. Retire from further tuning: `microstructure_acceleration` and `statistical_reversion` on
   M5 (largest losses, zero gross edge at every horizon); do not keep re-parameterising V1.
